The Vanto Method

Four connected capabilities. One recruitment strategy built around your study needs.

01

Targeted Advertising

We create digital advertising campaigns tailored to the trial's population, geography, recruitment goals, and intended audience.

02

Patient Qualification & Navigation

Our team screens prospective participants and supports follow-up, scheduling, and communication through site referral.

03

Person-to-Person Community Engagement

We extend recruitment through trusted healthcare relationships, reaching prospective participants through our network of pharmacists and physicians.

04

Creative & Educational Content

We create visual and audiovisual materials for recruitment and patient education, including ads, videos, brochures, and other study-support materials.

Expanded Community Access

Community Access Through ACHIEVE

Overview

Vanto's person-to-person community engagement is supported by ACHIEVE Clinical Expertise, expanding access through trusted healthcare professionals and community networks.

Approach

The model supports access to hard-to-reach and historically underrepresented patient populations through pharmacists, primary care physicians, and healthcare organizations.

Backed by

Founded by Christine Lee, PharmD, PhD, ACHIEVE is informed by her FDA experience in patient engagement, health equity, behavioral research, and research designed to inform regulatory decision-making.

Compliance

Clinician compensation is established in advance and is not contingent on patient interest, referrals, or enrollment.
